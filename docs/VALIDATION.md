- XCTest suite: 85 tests (43 core tests and 42 UI/bridge tests), including contact matching and address-only chats, immediate contact updates during refresh, tile resizing and minimum sizes, drag previews, draft preservation, stable bubble identities during confirmation — including file sends matched by attachment evidence (count, kind, name, size), identical files pairing in submission order, and refused messages that stay visible and claim no row — AppKit title-bar chrome, header close/focus/drag hit testing and press tracking, focus-chip selection, title-bar strip hit testing and non-movable-window dragging, rapid open/close churn, a fifth conversation replacing the least recently used tile in place, a quiet first launch that announces a lost connection once, keyboard traversal, the conversation list on the keyboard (⌘L) and the swipe detection that keeps row highlights off during a swipe, scroll pinning through content and viewport changes — appends that move nothing while reading, prepends that keep the same rows, viewport resizes that hold the top row, reflow anchoring by row identity, and the append/prepend classification itself — the shared zoom (clamping, persistence, old-workspace decoding, key classification, composer metrics in place), one-time tail animations (classification of appends, prepends, bursts and confirmations; the persisted preference), precomputed message rows (runs with one time stamp) and cached formatting, deleting a conversation from Mosaic, new-message tiles that address people and become the conversation on send, outgoing pictures and files (storing, staging for Messages' sandbox, pasteboard reading, ordered import slots that a send waits for, sending as their own bubbles through an injected fake transport, submission state separate from delivery, retry/reclaim/discard of refused sends, demo mode touching no transport), the retained read-only reader (data_version change detection on one connection, per-conversation history depth, reopening when the file is replaced, reactions/replies/edits/unsends from newer schemas, old fixtures without those columns), reactions (grouping, replacement, removal, idempotence, custom emoji, parts counted once), reply excerpts, reply originals fetched above the page and never from another chat, unsent messages that keep no words, unread counts from the seen boundary (repeated texts counted, sent messages, reactions and activity not; open and focus not reading), the New Messages line's placement, tiles opening on cached or prefetched history at once and single-conversation pages, the Photos grid's selection order, and saved-attachment name collisions. Run `./scripts/test.sh`; CI runs it on every push.
- Release archive packaging and ad-hoc signature verification: passed (verified before archiving and again after extracting outside the synced Desktop).
- Native Xcode project: build verified separately.
- Native demo view rendered and inspected at 1320 × 860 points in light and dark appearances, including floating Grid/Columns tiles and a synthetic SMS conversation to verify green outgoing bubbles.
- No animations: workspace changes are applied in transactions that disable animation, and the view tree disables implicit animation at its root. The former offscreen motion sequence was removed with the motion.
- Crash fix (0.2.1): five crash reports from 2026-10-02 showed `NSInternalInconsistencyException` raised from `-[NSWindow _postWindowNeedsUpdateConstraints]` during `NSHostingView.updateConstraints` (a nested hosting view five levels inside the title bar) while histories loaded and while tiles opened and closed quickly. The SwiftUI toolbar item was replaced by an empty AppKit toolbar, all `NSViewRepresentable`s now size from the layout proposal, focus requests are deferred out of SwiftUI updates, and `NSApplicationCrashOnExceptions` is off so a repeat would be logged, not fatal.
- Return, Shift–Return, independent demo sends, and AppleScript compilation tested without sending any real messages.

Tests use a synthetic SQLite database and fictional contacts. Real Contacts permissions and records, Messages history, transport behavior, and sending are not exercised by automated tests. Complete Full Disk Access, Contacts, and Automation setup, then test a conversation you choose before relying on the live bridge. Never use automated tests to send unsolicited messages.
