- **Dragging a tile is smooth, even with long conversations.** The tile you carry is now drawn as a picture of itself, so it follows the pointer without the app re-laying out its messages on every frame. When you let go, the tile springs into its new place as before.
- **The other tiles make way without redrawing.** Moving one tile no longer redraws all the others, so passing quickly over several tiles keeps up.
- **Messages that arrive while you drag wait until you drop.** A refresh no longer interrupts a drag. It applies the moment the tile lands.
