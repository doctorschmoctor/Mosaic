- **Scrolling far back stays smooth.** However far up a conversation you go, about the same number of messages are laid out, so scrolling no longer gets choppier the further back you are, and older history starts loading well before you reach it.
- **Latest is instant from anywhere.** The newest messages and their photos stay ready while you read far back, so Latest, or dragging the scroller to the end, shows them at once instead of rebuilding every message in between.
- **No more stalls far back.** Resizing a tile, zooming, or jumping to a search match while reading far back no longer rebuilds every message down to the newest.
- **Photos you've already seen show at once** when you scroll back to them, instead of after a spinner.
