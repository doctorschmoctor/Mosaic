- **Group chats show their photo.** Mosaic now reads the photo Messages keeps for each group, so group conversations show their picture in the sidebar and in their tile instead of the generic group icon.
- **Switching conversations in Focus is instant.** Tabbing or clicking between conversations no longer blinks: each one is shown as you left it, already scrolled and with its pictures loaded, and it keeps its place and draft while you switch.
- **Scrolling back through a conversation is smoother.** Older messages load well before you reach the top, a few at a time, so you no longer wait at the top for more history. New messages that arrive while you scroll wait until you stop, and loading history in one tile no longer redraws the others.
