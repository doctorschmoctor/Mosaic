- **Unread follows what you actually read.** A conversation stays unread until you open it, or click or Tab into its tile. New messages in an open tile you aren't in now count as unread and show in the Unread list. Closing a tile with × or ⇧⌘W no longer marks it read. Opening several unread conversations one after another now clears every one of them from the list.
- **The resize keys move the tile's edge the way the arrow points.** With ⌃⌥⌘, a right-hand tile now widens with ← and narrows with →, a left-hand tile the other way round, and a bottom-row tile grows with ↑. The Workspace menu shows the right keys for the tile you're in.
- **Tiles always find their even split again.** Each resize press lands on fixed steps from the middle, so pressing back towards it always ends exactly in the middle. A divider dragged near the middle snaps to it.
