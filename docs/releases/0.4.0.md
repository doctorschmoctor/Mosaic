- **Closing a tile works again.** The tiles left move into its place at once. Since the menus started showing which tile actions were available, closing or reopening a tile could leave the workspace drawn as it was before. The menus now catch up after each change instead of during it.
- **Find in Conversation** (⌥⌘F) keeps up with your typing and searches the conversation's older history by itself. Choosing a match closes the find bar and opens the thread on that message.
- **Conversation Details** (⌘I) lists the people, photos and videos, files and links from the whole history. Esc goes back to the tile.
- **Notifications, if you want them:** turn them on in Settings › Notifications. You get a notification for a new message in a conversation you can't see; choose name only or name and message, and mute a conversation for a while from its tile or sidebar row.
- **Unread and Drafts filters** in the sidebar (⌃⌘1–3), and Go to Next Unread (⌥⌘U). Going back to All is instant.
- **Pin to Top** in the sidebar, and **Protect from Replacement** for tiles you never want closed to make room.
- **Hide from Mosaic** (swipe a row, or its menu), with Undo and a Hidden Conversations list to bring one back. It stays in Messages.
- **Reopen Closed Tile** (⇧⌘T) brings a tile back with its draft. Drafts are kept when you close a tile and listed under Drafts.
- **Tiles from the keyboard:** Move Tile Left/Right (⌥⌘←/→), make the focused tile wider, narrower, taller or shorter (⌃⌥⌘ arrows), and Equal Tile Sizes.
- **Files in the message field:** Quick Look, progress while a photo is imported, Stop and Retry.
- **New messages show where they will go** (iMessage or SMS, and to whom) before you send.
- **Long threads stay quick:** a tile lays out its newest messages and older ones as you scroll up, so resizing and switching layouts no longer slow down with history.
- **Settings** has Connection, Appearance & Input, Notifications, and Privacy & Data tabs.
